iterami/Docs.htm/common HTM Repos/webgl.js Prefabs/prefabs_webgl_lines_tree(args)
  • Creates a lines tree prefab with the specified properties.
  • Position is the center of the bottom of the trunk.
  • Args will be used in entity creation after being cleaned up by webgl_prefab_args(args).

trunk_color [.4, .2, 0, 1] array of numbers
trunk_count_max 10 number
trunk_count_min 1 number
trunk_length 10 number
trunk_width_max 2 number
