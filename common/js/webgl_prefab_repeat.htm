  • Prefab used to randomly place repeated copies of another prefab within an area.
  • If the "characters" arg is not false, new characters will be created for each prefab and will inherit the specified properties.
  • You can repeat entities without using a prefab by setting the "type" arg to "webgl_entity_create". The "properties" will then be passed to webgl_entity_create(args) as its args.
